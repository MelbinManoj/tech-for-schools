# 🖥️ Tech for Schools — Service Project

> Community service project: restoring unused computers and teaching primary students hardware and engineering basics.

## 📖 Overview

A **community service project** focused on bringing technology and engineering to primary-school students.

## ❓ Problem

Many schools have computers sitting unused and students with little exposure to how technology works.

## ✨ What We Did

- Trained students on the basics of computer hardware and programs
- Gave primary-level students a glimpse of engineering through mini-projects
- Restored unused computers at both hardware and software level

## ⚙️ Approach

1. Identified unused or faulty computers
2. Repaired hardware and reinstalled/updated software
3. Ran hands-on sessions on computer basics
4. Demonstrated simple engineering mini-projects

## 🛠️ Skills

`Hardware Repair` `Software Troubleshooting` `Teaching` `Knowledge Sharing`
